General instructions. The assignment is completed in groups of two to three students. You may choose your group members. Once the group is formed, select one
paper from the list below and confirm your choice by e-mail no later than September
15, at 5 pm.
No more than two groups may work on the same paper. Papers will therefore be
allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. Groups that have not selected a paper by
the deadline will be assigned both a group and a paper.
Each group must complete three tasks:
1. One-page policy summary. Write a one-page summary of the paper aimed at
an informed but non-specialist audience, following the style of the IZA World of
Labor. An example is available here.
The summary should explain the research question, empirical strategy, main
findings, and policy relevance without reproducing the abstract or other parts
of the paper.
2. Lithuanian application (500–1,000 words). Discuss whether and how the findings of the paper are relevant for Lithuania. Your argument must be supported
by data, institutional evidence, and/or relevant research. Simply stating that
Lithuania is “similar” or “different” from the country studied in the paper is not
sufficient.

empirical methods discussed in the course;

abstract, introduction, or conclusion;

setting matter for applying the results to Lithuania?

• communicate professionally: slides should be clear and readable, the presentation should respect the time limit, and arguments should be concise and evidencebased;
